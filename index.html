Install on iPhone

Host this folder online, open the URL in Safari, tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen. After that it launches like an app and works offline after first load.

Controls: tap Swing / Pitch. Use Contact Swing for safer contact and less power.

Included: balls, strikes, walks, strikeouts, singles, doubles, triples, home runs, errors, double plays, saved settings, offline cache, app icon, full-screen PWA mode.
